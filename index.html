        Automation

        Bought with Royal Jelly on the Nuptial tab. Each one can be switched off here.

        Share of the colony each caste should hold. Standing Orders lays whichever caste sits furthest below its share, and a caste set to zero is never laid to meet a target.

        Two exceptions, both deliberate. Excavators are dug whenever the nest is nearly full, whatever their share says — a colony that cannot grow is the one case a ratio gets wrong, and that is why zero is the right number for them. And once every share is met the surplus goes to foragers, so they are laid whether or not you give them one.

        Measured over five hours: 80% foragers, 5% nurses, 8% soldiers, and nothing for excavators. Soldiers under 7% win the early raids and lose the late ones, because monsters grow with every win and with the colony; over 10% never loses one and costs you the ants that would have fed it. Foragers below about 85% change nothing at all — they naturally sit near that share, so a target they already meet never wins.

        Nothing is automated yet. The Royal Lineage sells it, on the Nuptial tab.
